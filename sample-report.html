What automated scanning cannot check

Automated engines reliably detect roughly half of WCAG issues. Full conformance also depends on the checks below, which require a human reviewer — they are flagged here so this report never overstates what a scan alone proves.
Manual checkWhy it matters

Focus visibility & orderA visible focus indicator following a logical sequence (WCAG 2.4.3, 2.4.7)
Alt-text qualityA scan verifies alt text exists — a human verifies it is meaningful (WCAG 1.1.1)
Media captions & transcriptsAccuracy of captions on video/audio content (WCAG 1.2.x)
Motion, zoom & reflowContent usable at 200% zoom and 320px width without loss (WCAG 1.4.4, 1.4.10)

Scope & honesty note: this is an automated conformance scan using axe-core, the industry-standard open-source engine.
