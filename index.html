The honest part

No scan makes you "compliant," and neither does a widget. Automated tools catch roughly half of WCAG issues; the rest need human judgment — which is why our Professional and Compliance tiers include manual review, and why our reports list exactly what a scan can't check. Roughly a quarter of recent ADA suits were filed against sites that already had an accessibility overlay installed. We will never sell you a certification, a guarantee against lawsuits, or legal advice — we sell you an accurate technical picture and a fix list.

Questions worth asking anyone selling you an audit

Will this make my site "ADA compliant"?

No one can honestly promise that — there is no official ADA certification for websites, and no audit or widget prevents someone from filing a claim. What we deliver is a technical assessment against WCAG 2.1 AA, the standard courts and regulators reference, plus the exact fix list. Whether that meets your legal obligations is a question for your attorney.

What do you need from me to start?

Just your website address, and which pages matter most to you (checkout, booking, contact). No logins, no code access, no install. Audits of pages behind a login are possible with a test account you create for the engagement.

Can you also fix what you find?

Yes — remediation is quoted after the audit, from the actual findings ($125/hr or a fixed package), so you never pay to fix problems you don't have. You're equally free to hand the report to your own developer; it's written for exactly that.

Why are your prices below the $1,500–$7,000 agencies charge?

The scan pipeline is automated end-to-end and the reports generate themselves, so you pay for the judgment — the manual keyboard and screen-reader review, the prioritization, the fix guidance — not for hours of tooling work. Same WCAG 2.1 AA standard, less overhead.

How fast do you reply?

Within one business day, usually much faster. Starter audits deliver in 3 business days.
